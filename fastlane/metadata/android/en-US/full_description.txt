Take a few minutes to move. Warm up before a run, stretch after a workout, wind down at night, or try a short bodyweight strength routine. An animated guide and a timer show you what to do. Tap an exercise to preview the movement and read a short guide.

Earn fibers as you go, open daily chests, and find little creatures called Fibbies. Collect them in your album and merge matching Fibbies to see them evolve.

A little Fibbie greets you with a fresh thought when you open the app. Tap it for a stretch and a few kind words.

Your progress stays on your device. You can export a backup code from Settings.
